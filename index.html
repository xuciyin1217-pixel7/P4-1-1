Sentence Transformation

  1. Peter gets along well with people, so he has many friends.
    ➔ Peter gets along well with people, so he has friends.
  2. Father ran into much trouble when he was still a little boy.
    ➔ Father ran into trouble when he was still a little boy.
  3. Bill is always thinking of making much money in the future.
    ➔ Bill is always thinking of making money in the future.
  4. In general, we have much rain in August here.
    ➔ In general, we have rain in August here.
